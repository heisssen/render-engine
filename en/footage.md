**Attribution required** = text that MUST go in the YouTube description. 'none' = public domain; a courtesy line is still good practice, e.g. *Archive footage: Bundesarchiv, DFF, Cinematek (Royal Film Archive of Belgium), Austrian Film Museum via Europeana; Wikimedia Commons; US National Archives; Library of Congress.*

## Rights doubts (read before use)

- **i_hitler_list_regiment_1916.jpg:** Commons tags it PD (anonymous), but the source is an autograph auction and the photographer is unidentified. Many Hitler WWI photos are Heinrich Hoffmann prints (Hoffmann died 1957, so not PD in Germany until 2028). Medium risk.
- **i_tolkien_1916.jpg:** PD-anon on Commons. The Tolkien Estate is protective. Low-medium risk; low-res.
- **i_hemingway_milan_1918.jpg:** unknown photographer; first known publication 1978 (Buckley). JFK Library Hemingway photos often say "copyright unknown". Medium risk.
- **a_berlin_unter_den_linden_1914.jpg:** anonymous 1914 photo, first published in a 1991 book (per Commons). Low-medium risk.
- **h_german_curse_in_russia_1917.ogg, d_kleinschmidt_austria_1915.ogg, d_battles_of_a_nation_1915.ogg, d_dawson_russian_trenches_1915.ogg, g_mackensen_constantinople_1916.ogg:** the archival film is PD (1915-18 US releases / German newsreels), but the Commons files are modern re-edits by Ron van Dopperen (text slides, possibly music). Use only the archival frames. Mute the audio. Battles of a Nation carries burned-in LoC timecode.
- **f_lusitania_passengers_1915.webm:** the uploader added modern sound. Mute it.
- **b_nicholas_ii_parade_1912.ogv, k_baltic_fleet_pre1917.ogv, h_lenin_trotsky_1918.ogv:** sourced from a Russian YouTube channel (Rossiya582) with a channel logo and added tint. The footage is pre-1917 or 1918 newsreel (PD), but crop the logo. Content ID could still claim restorations; keep the Commons page links as proof.
- **Europeana / Bundesarchiv items:** Public Domain Mark set by the archive, so they are fine. Several carry archive bugs (Bundesarchiv, CINEMATEK, Filmmuseum, DFF). Cropping is allowed. Keeping the bug is also fine.
- **Rejected on purpose:** IWM-sourced Commons copies (Berlin 1 Aug 1914 crowd photos Q81755/Q81739, London crowd Q81832, Churchill CH4778), a Getty/Hulton-sourced Kaiser and Tsar photo, a Churchill photo traced to a Getty ID, British Pathé, Periscope Film (CC BY-NC-ND), and Kitchener's Great Army (IWM film).
- **LoC website (loc.gov):** returned HTTP 403 to automated requests, so LoC material was taken from Commons mirrors (Bain / LoC scans), which carry "No known restrictions".
